# Transcribed image-only policy content

The tables and figures below appear in the RideWise policy PDFs **only as scanned images**, so PDF
text extraction cannot read them. They are transcribed here verbatim so they can be searched and
cited. The original PDFs remain the authoritative source and are unmodified.

## RW-POL-03 (Cancellation & Refund Policy, Version 2) — Annexure 1: Refund schedule (Version 2)

Source: RW-POL-03, page 2, scanned signed copy.
Applies to bookings made on or after 1 July 2026, 00:00 IST.

| Cancellation before scheduled pickup | Refund of rental charge |
|---|---|
| 48 hours or more | 100% |
| 12 hours or more, but less than 48 hours | 75% |
| 2 hours or more, but less than 12 hours | 25% |
| Less than 2 hours | No refund (0%) |

Cancelled by RideWise: 100% refund in all cases.
No-show: no refund.
Approved: RideWise Policy Committee, 20 June 2026.

<a id="rw-pol-04-annexure-a"></a>
## RW-POL-04 (Late Return & Damage Penalty Schedule) — Annexure A: Tier benefits

Source: RW-POL-04, page 3, scanned signed copy.
Late return grace period by customer tier.

| Customer tier | Grace period on late return |
|---|---|
| STANDARD | None |
| SILVER | None |
| GOLD | 30 minutes (up to and including 30 min) |

A GOLD return up to 30 minutes late: no late fee.
A GOLD return more than 30 minutes late: the full lateness is charged from the first minute, as for other tiers.
Approved: RideWise Policy Committee, 15 December 2024.

## RW-POL-05 (EV User Guide & FAQ) — Figure 1: How to charge a RideWise EV

Source: RW-POL-05, page 1, figure.

1. Park at a RideWise hub or approved charger
2. Switch off the vehicle
3. Connect the RideWise charging cable
4. Wait for the green light on the dashboard
5. Unplug and stow the cable under the seat

## RW-POL-05 (EV User Guide & FAQ) — Figure 2: Battery level at return and the charging fee

Source: RW-POL-05, page 2, figure.

| Band | Battery level at return | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| RED | Below 10% | ₹150 low-battery charging fee applies |
| AMBER | 10% – 19% | No fee, but please charge before your next trip |
| GREEN | 20% and above | Ideal return level |

Exactly 10% is not below 10%, so no fee is charged.
